Some places hold a little piece of the city.
Blue Hills is one of them.

A spot remembered for its vibrance, its people, and nights that brought Hartford together. For owner Lorie, bringing that spirit back is personal.

Between nursing and running the bar, she wears plenty of hats. At the heart of them all is the same thing: caring for people.

Her vision is to keep the welcome warm, make room for the neighborhood, and give Hartford its go-to spot again.
