Accessibility Statement

Help accessing the Blue Hills Cafe website, information, and inquiry options.

Last updated October 8, 2026

Our aim

We want visitors with disabilities to be able to find information and contact Blue Hills Cafe. We use the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2, Level AA as a reference for improvements. This statement is not a claim of complete conformance or an independent accessibility certification.

Features provided

  • Keyboard-operable navigation and controls, with visible focus indicators.
  • A skip link on the main website pages to move directly to the content.
  • Text alternatives for meaningful images and labels for inquiry fields.
  • Responsive layouts and support for browser zoom.
  • Reduced-motion support for scroll animations.
  • A special-offer dialog that can be dismissed using its close button or the Escape key.

Help with an inquiry

The inquiry form opens an email draft and requires an email application. If this does not work with your device or assistive technology, you can contact the cafe directly by phone or email. You do not need to use the form to ask about catering, events, or rentals.

Report an accessibility barrier

Please tell us which page or feature caused difficulty, what you were trying to do, and how you would like us to contact you. If helpful, include your browser and assistive technology. You do not need to disclose a disability or medical information.

Email an accessibility concern or call (860) 436-1553. Let us know if you need information in another format.

External services and venue access

Linked services, including maps and email applications, may have different accessibility features. Contact us if they prevent you from getting information or making an inquiry.

This statement concerns the website. For questions about physical access, seating, or accommodations at the cafe, contact the team before your visit so you can discuss your needs.
