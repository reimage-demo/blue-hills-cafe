How information is handled when you browse this website or contact Blue Hills Cafe.

Scope and contact

This policy covers the Blue Hills Cafe website and its landing page. It describes the site as configured on the last-updated date above. It does not describe every practice of external services you choose to use.

Information you choose to send

The event inquiry form asks for your name, email address, inquiry type, and optionally your preferred date, estimated guest count, bottle requests, and event details.

Selecting “Create email inquiry” prepares a draft in your email application. The site does not send the inquiry to a server or save it in a website database. You must send the email yourself. Your email application may store the draft under its own settings.

If you send an email or call, the cafe receives the information you provide to discuss your request, respond to questions, and arrange services. Avoid including payment card details, identity documents, or other sensitive information in the form or ordinary email.

Browser storage and tracking

The main site uses browser session storage to remember that the Thursday special has been shown in the current tab session. This flag contains no inquiry details. You can clear it through your browser settings; clearing it may cause the special to appear again.

The website code does not set advertising cookies or include analytics trackers, advertising pixels, account registration, or payment processing. Browser and hosting services may handle technical data as described below.

Hosting, fonts, and external services

GitHub Pages hosts this site. When your browser requests a page or asset, the hosting service receives technical information such as your IP address and request details. Read the GitHub Privacy Statement for its practices.

Typography is loaded from Google Fonts. Your browser contacts Google to request font files, which shares technical request information. See Google Fonts’ privacy information.

Directions links open Google Maps. Email and phone links open your chosen applications. Those services handle information under their own policies. Appispot booking is not connected to this site at present; rental buttons lead to an inquiry section.

Correspondence, retention, and your choices

Sent inquiries are handled through the cafe’s email service rather than a website database. Copies may remain in the cafe’s mailbox and your email provider’s systems. The website does not control those providers’ retention settings.

You can contact the cafe to ask about information you sent, request a correction, or request deletion. Include only enough information to identify the inquiry. Any applicable identity checks, recordkeeping obligations, or other legal requirements may affect how a request can be fulfilled.

The website has no data-sale or targeted-advertising integration. This policy does not make promises about the independent practices of hosting, font, mapping, or email providers; review their policies when using their services.

Children and sensitive information

This site is intended for people seeking information about the cafe, catering, and events. The inquiry form is not intended for children under 13. If a child has sent personal information, contact the cafe to request that the message be reviewed and removed where appropriate.

Changes to this policy

If website features or information handling change, this page should be updated. The last-updated date identifies this version. Contact the cafe with questions about its current practices.
